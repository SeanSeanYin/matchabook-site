Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

The short version: Matchabook collects none of your data. Everything you record stays on your own device. We run no servers, and nothing is ever transmitted anywhere.

What we store, and where

  • Your matcha records — tins, usage logs, photos, and notes are stored in a local database on your iPhone only.
  • Sign in with Apple — the app receives only the anonymous identifier Apple provides (and your name, if you choose to share it). This is stored on your device only and is never sent anywhere.
  • Photos — only photos you actively pick are saved into the app's local database. The app cannot access the rest of your photo library.

What we don't do

  • No developer servers — your data is never uploaded, backed up, or synced by us
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No data sharing with any third party

Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely by your device. No server is involved.

Export and deletion

You can export your usage records as a CSV file from within the app (exported files are yours to manage). Deleting a tin or record removes it from the local database; uninstalling the app deletes all data.

In-app purchases

Matchabook Pro purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment information.

Changes to this policy

If future features (such as cloud sync) require changes to this policy, we will update this page and note the new effective date.
